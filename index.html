Who we are

A young company with the discipline of a giant.

Founded in 2007, La Renon climbed into India's top pharmaceutical tier within a single decade — an ascent powered by scientific integrity, deep therapeutic focus and an uncompromising standard of care.

  • Specialty first. Purpose-built portfolios in nephrology, critical care and neuroscience.
  • Vertically integrated. Discovery, formulation and manufacturing under one roof.
  • Globally trusted. Registrations and partnerships spanning 40+ markets.
